Questions clients ask.

How much does a website cost?

The price depends on what you need. We negotiate it on a call or by text, and agree it before work starts.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the site. You get a time estimate on our call, before anything starts.

Do I need a domain and hosting?

For a professional address like yourbusiness.co.ke, yes. I'll explain the options and costs on the call so you know exactly what you're paying for.

Can customers contact me through WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp buttons go in the key places on your site, with a message already typed for the customer.

Can you improve my existing website?

Yes. I can redesign pages, make them work better on phones, and add new sections or features.

What happens after launch?

You can ask for updates and changes later. We agree what's included before the project starts.

Clear terms before we start.

No surprises: we confirm the scope and price together before I begin.

Scope and price

Before work starts, we agree in writing on the pages/features, total price, what is not included, and the payment schedule.

Content and timing

You provide or approve your business details, text, photos, prices and contact information. The timeline starts once the agreed content is ready; delays in receiving content can move the delivery date.

Revisions and approval

We agree how many review rounds are included before starting. You review the site on a phone or computer and approve the final version before launch.

Domain, hosting and third-party costs

Any domain, paid hosting, email service, paid plugin or other third-party cost is explained separately and approved by you before purchase. These costs are not automatically included in the build price.

After launch

Ongoing updates, maintenance, new features and support are agreed separately unless they are explicitly included in the original written scope.
